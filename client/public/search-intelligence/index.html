Puphr Search Intelligence

Data-driven market research and product development.

Puphr Search Intelligence is an internal research and analytics platform operated by Puphr LLC to support software development, market analysis, search engine optimization and advertising performance evaluation.

What the platform does

  • Researches historical keyword search volumes and trends.
  • Identifies emerging market opportunities and unmet customer needs.
  • Analyzes authorized advertising and organic search performance.
  • Informs product planning and generates internal analytical reports.

Access

Access is limited to authorized Puphr LLC personnel and systems. The platform is not offered as a public service. This page explains its purpose and data practices; it does not provide a public sign-in.
