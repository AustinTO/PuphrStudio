Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

Puphr Search Intelligence is an internal research and analytics platform operated by Puphr LLC.

Information accessed

Depending on the authorized integrations enabled, the platform may access keyword search volumes, historical search metrics, advertising account identifiers and performance statistics, Search Console website performance data, and geographic search trends. Access is limited to authorized accounts and information needed for the documented functions.

How information is used

Information is used for internal market research, product development, advertising analysis, search optimization and business planning. Google user data is not sold or used to create unrelated advertising profiles.

Storage and security

Research information may be stored in access-controlled cloud infrastructure operated by or on behalf of Puphr LLC. The platform is designed to keep authentication credentials separate from research records and restrict access to authorized personnel and systems.

Automated analysis and sharing

Authorized automated research systems may process search metrics and aggregated analytical information for internal reports and recommendations. Google user data will not be used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine learning models. Google user data is not made publicly available. Any processing by service providers must be necessary for the documented functions and comply with applicable Google API policies.

Retention, deletion and revocation

Information is retained only as necessary for the documented purposes. Authorized account holders may request deletion of stored information by contacting us at the address below. Google Account holders may revoke OAuth access through their Google Account connections settings; revocation does not automatically delete previously stored records.

Google API compliance

Puphr Search Intelligence's use and transfer to any other application of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements where applicable.
